    Personal engineering study

    From repetitive order review to governed decision support

    This POC studies where deterministic policy, bounded LLM reasoning, human approval, and production observability should meet. All records are synthetic and every workflow remains read-only.

    01

    What to learn

    Compare repeatable rules with an LLM that selects read-only tools and returns a schema-constrained proposal.

    02

    What stays controlled

    Classification, severity, allowed actions, and approval requirements remain authoritative application policy.

    03

    What to inspect

    Use metrics, correlated traces, and decision audits to understand behavior rather than treating the agent as a black box.

    Potential real-world value

    Who could benefit?

    In a governed pilot, the workflow could reduce time-to-diagnosis and make triage more consistent. These are hypotheses to measure with real operational data, not results claimed by this synthetic demo.

    Order plannersPrioritized exceptions, evidence, and a proposed next step.
    Procurement teamsConsistent supplier recovery and escalation context.
    Inventory teamsEarlier visibility into shortages and transfer options.
    Customer operationsExplainable delivery-risk summaries for follow-up.
    Control-tower leadersStandardized severity, workload signals, and decision lineage.
    Engineering and riskA reference pattern for tools, guardrails, tests, and auditability.

    Production direction

    A safe adoption path

    1. Connect a read-only ERP source and calibrate rules.
    2. Evaluate silently against historical and live planner decisions.
    3. Add identity, durable storage, approvals, and OpenTelemetry export.
    4. Pilot one exception family before considering controlled execution.
